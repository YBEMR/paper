ARTICLE                  INFO                               ABSTRACT

Keywords:                                                   Heterogeneous systems-on-chip (SoCs) integrate diverse cores with different performance and energy tradeoffs.
Scheduling                                                  Scheduling applications with soft deadline constraints is highly complex in such heterogeneous platforms, and
Energy-efficient                                            the complexity is further exacerbated by the streaming jobs generated by applications from domains such as
Soft deadline
                                                            communication and radar systems. Existing deadline-aware schedulers typically first translate the job deadlines
Heterogeneous SoC
                                                            to task-level slacks before scheduling, which is the time available for a processing element (PE) to execute a
Domain-specific SoC
                                                            specific task. Task-level slacks are critically dependent on the task-to-PE allocation of all other tasks from the
                                                            same job (intra-job) or concurrent jobs (inter-job). However, this allocation is usually unknown before the start
                                                            of the scheduling process. To address the problem, we propose PED, a probabilistic energy-efficient deadline-
                                                            aware scheduler for heterogeneous SoCs. PED minimizes the average tardiness of streaming jobs with the least
                                                            energy consumption by accurately predicting the task-to-PE allocation using Neural Network and considering
                                                            intra- and inter-job contentions when scheduling tasks. Our extensive experimental results in a domain-specific
                                                            SoC (DSSoC) designed for radar and communication domains show that PED can reduce tardiness by 6.9× with
                                                            comparable energy consumption; and reduce energy consumption by 14% without any loss in tardiness, when
                                                            compared with state-of-the-art schedulers.
