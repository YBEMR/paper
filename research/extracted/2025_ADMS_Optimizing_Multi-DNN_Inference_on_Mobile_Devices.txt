   We implemented ADMS using the TFLite [10] framework,

high compatibility. TFLite enables the conversion and optimiza-

mobile devices, facilitating efficient inference through ADMS.

inference, and task queue management and scheduling. These
APIs seamlessly integrate with TFLite’s APIs, enabling ADMS
to directly interact with the TFLite framework for streamlined
model inference.
