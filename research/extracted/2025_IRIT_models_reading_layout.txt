Hardware and application aware performance, power and energy models for
modern HPC servers with DVFS
Georges Da Costa
IRIT, Université de Toulouse, CNRS, Toulouse, France



ARTICLE               INFO                                ABSTRACT

Keywords:                                                 Energy usage and its ecological impact is now a major concern in High Performance Computing (HPC). To
Performance                                               optimize supercomputers efficiency, researchers rely on models, as accessing actual platform is complex and
Power                                                     costly. Changing DVFS (Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling) is the most studied method, but it impacts
Energy
                                                          power, performance and energy in a complex way.
Model
                                                              We propose to bridge the gap between the theoretical and the practical approaches. We propose a
HPC
                                                          multi cluster, multi application model accurately describing from a theoretical point of view the power and
                                                          performance of applications subject to DVFS. We show how to use it on a runtime system with a minimal
                                                          overhead, using only a few hardware performance counters and RAPL (Running Average Power Limit).
                                                              We validate our models using an extensive dataset, obtained using 18 different clusters and running 9
                                                          benchmarks. We also show how such model can be used to optimize the energy-to-solution for HPC workload.
