Notes:
  - uncoupled: same task/power/deadline, only g1N=g2N=0 with G
    rebuilt from the original network definition (diagonal g1+g12 etc.).
  - In the uncoupled model the NPU loses its two extra heat paths to the
    CPU cores; if E_ss* is Inf there, no schedule passes the steady check
    (NPU steady equilibrium with B2 exceeds the limit) while transient
    schedules remain feasible.
  - no-dependency: theoretical ablation only; added schedules are NOT
    executable in a real inference pipeline.
